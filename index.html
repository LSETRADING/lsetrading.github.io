What we do

An elite pipeline to the trading floor

The Trading Society exists to take students from every background at LSE and hand them the vocabulary, the reps, and the network to step onto a real trading floor. No prior finance degree required, only ambition.

Our subcommittees run the year like a training programme. Members learn markets from the inside through weekly workshops, live trading simulations, testimonies from practitioners across the buy side and sell side, and classes that go from first principles up to the maths that desks actually use.

By the end of the year, members leave with a full year's worth of preparation for spring weeks, summer internships, and full time seats, and more importantly the community of traders they trained alongside.

Research

Members publish equity deep dives, sector primers, and multi-week research projects that teach the markets by doing.

Simulations

Society-run simulations put members through market making, buy side allocation, and full crisis events across the trading floor.

Speakers

The people who matter: MDs, portfolio managers and senior traders, explaining in their words how to reach the top.

Networking Events

The biggest firms come to the society to recruit, a direct funnel from the committee room to their front office desks.

Equities

Our Equities division trains analysts to dissect a company's fundamentals and confidently argue an investment thesis in front of others. Working alongside the Head of Equities, you build the foundation in Autumn Term: fundamental and technical analysis, valuation through DCF and peer comparables, and a working understanding of how different markets behave. Winter Term is where you apply it. Analysts construct full stock pitches, defend them under scrutiny, and test their calls through chart simulations. The strongest pitches enter the society's discretionary simulated equities book, so your research carries a real position and a real result behind it.

Macro

Our Macro division gives analysts experience in analysing global macroeconomic trading strategies. You will learn how currencies, government bonds and interest rates move in response to inflation, growth and global events, through weekly one hour classes delivered by the division heads. We cover the function of a macro desk, the uses of FX markets and the role of central banks, before building towards debating your own macro views and expressing them as trade ideas in the subcommittee trading book. No prior knowledge is required. We are looking for students with a real interest which they can demonstrate.

Derivatives

Ready to actually be prepared for the trading floor? Join the Derivatives Subcommittee. You'll become part of an elite group of students where no prior experience or specific degree is required, just ambition. Don't worry, it's not calculus. Every asset is actually traded using derivatives, so this is where you learn how markets work in the real world. Level up through classes, interactive workshops, and competitions with unique prizes, and get a real edge on your career with spring week and internship prep through mock interviews and live trading simulations. And it's fun, trust us: a tight-knit, motivated crew with socials all year round. Forget the rest: this is the real stuff, and this is where it starts. Join us!

Commodities

Commodities are the physical goods the world runs on: oil, gas, metals and crops. This subcommittee is about how they get priced and traded. Across the term we look at why prices move, how supply and demand get tracked, and how traders build a view on a market and act on it, including the contracts used to trade commodities across energy, metals and agriculture. We also run events with people working in the industry. More to come soon.

Emerging Markets

Our Emerging Markets division gives analysts the chance to broaden the geographic reach of their commercial awareness and build a solid technical foundation. You will cover BRICS economies, Southeast Asia, Latin America, Central and Eastern Europe, the Middle East and Africa, following the developments that move these markets on a weekly basis. The focus of this subcommittee is practical. You will develop expertise in currency positioning, sovereign credit analysis and equity research in developing economies, alongside an understanding of the local dynamics. Expect weekly homework and open discussions. The term closes with an internal pitch, where you work in groups of two or three over a week to build a trade idea and present it to the subcommittee. Analysts leave with a strong read of emerging markets and a pitch that is ready for interview.

Credit

Our Credit division offers analysts a direct route into the full capital structure, spanning investment grade bonds, high yield, leveraged loans and fast growing private credit. You work closely with our Head of Credit to build, present and defend sophisticated relative value trades, pitching long and short ideas on real issuers and pricing risk the way an institutional desk would. The strongest ideas each week are taken into a live credit book that competes directly against the other divisions.

Advanced Trader · Equities

The Advanced Traders Programme (Equities) is for second year, third year and master's students looking to apply their skills in equity and equity derivatives research and execution. Members undertake weekly research, modelling and thesis formulation, which is then presented to the cohort. We trade a portfolio of our highest conviction ideas, publish our best research as it lands, and release a monthly State of the Market article alongside our FICC counterparts on the most pressing trends in the market as a whole. We aim to compete, and win, in as many competitions as possible, bringing high quality differentiated analysis whenever we represent the Trading Society.

Eligibility The Advanced Traders Programme is not open to first year students. Applications are for second year, third year and master's students only.

Advanced Trader · FICC

The FICC Advanced Traders Programme builds a research driven approach while giving comprehensive exposure to Fixed Income, Currencies and Commodities across emerging and developed markets. Analysts develop expertise in global macro, learning the interconnected nature of these products alongside extensive training in the pricing and use of practical financial derivatives. Analysts regularly develop and present trading ideas, contribute to analytical discussions, and follow current events and their effects on markets. Working alongside the Equities programme, analysts contribute to a broader understanding of markets through shared research and the monthly State of the Market publication, bringing together perspectives across asset classes. The programme develops independent thinkers who can form their own market views, articulate their rationale, and turn them into actionable trades.

Eligibility The Advanced Traders Programme is not open to first year students. Applications are for second year, third year and master's students only.

Events

The Events subcommittee organises everything the society puts on, from speaker sessions and industry evenings to socials and trading simulations. Members lead outreach to firms and speakers across the sector, from bulge bracket trading desks to proprietary trading firms and hedge funds, and take each event from initial contact through to delivery. We are looking for committed and well organised students who are enthusiastic about creating memorable events and who bring fresh ideas to how they are designed and run. Along the way, members can interview the speakers they help bring in, build a strong professional network across the industry, and learn directly from practitioners working in the field.
